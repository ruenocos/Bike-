# Credits

## Audio
All sound effects in `audio/sfx/` are synthesized from scratch by
`audio/tools/make_sfx.py` (noise, filters, sine tones). No third-party
samples; no licence obligations. Regenerate with `python -I audio/tools/make_sfx.py`.
